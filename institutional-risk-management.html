ACADEMIC INTEREST

Institutional Risk Management

Risk-aware organisational practice

Overview

Risk management is a broad area of academic and professional interest concerned with how institutions recognise uncertainty, consider consequences and support responsible decision-making.

Areas of interest include organisational awareness, communication, learning and the practical challenges of sustaining risk-aware routines in public and development-oriented organisations.

This page is a general field overview. It does not present a proprietary framework, research finding, unpublished method or manuscript content.
