Research programme 01

IRMRA–DSL

Institutional Risk Management Readiness Architecture & Developmental Success Logic

A conceptual framework linking institutional risk management readiness and risk management effectiveness with developmental success reasoning. Its development draws on an earlier empirical research context in Egypt (2013–2016), while later theoretical elaboration is distinguished from that original evidence.

Public overview · no full manuscript
Research programme 02

DIR Architecture Theory

Developmental Institutional Readiness

A proposed architecture for examining how institutional building units, higher-order pillars and developmental institutional culture relate to institutional readiness for developmental action. Further empirical assessment is required before operational use as a standardised decision instrument.

Conceptual framework · validation required
Research programme 03

SDIS

Student Development Intelligence System

An exploratory university-governed concept for supporting student development across the student lifecycle, with attention to institutional governance, appropriate data practices and student participation. Technical design details are not publicly disclosed.

High-level concept only
