ACADEMIC INTEREST

Institutional Development

Institutions, capabilities and development practice

Overview

Institutional development is concerned with how organisations structure responsibilities, coordinate their activities and strengthen their capacity to respond to social and developmental needs.

Themes of interest include governance, organisational learning, collaboration, resource use and the relationship between institutions and the communities they serve.

This page is a general description of academic interests, not a statement of a new theoretical model, assessment instrument or unpublished research proposition.
